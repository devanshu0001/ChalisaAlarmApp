Chalisa Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Chalisa Alarm is designed to help users wake up and complete a Hanuman Chalisa recitation before dismissing their alarm.

Information we collect

Chalisa Alarm does not require you to create an account in Version 1.

Alarm settings, repeat days, onboarding status, and streak information are stored locally on your device by the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

Chalisa Alarm requests microphone access so it can listen while you recite and verify your progress through the Hanuman Chalisa.

The app does not intentionally save recordings of your recitation. Speech recognition is performed using the Android speech-recognition service available on your device. Depending on your device and configured speech-recognition provider, audio or speech data may be processed by that provider and may be subject to the provider's own privacy policy and terms.

Notifications and alarms

The app may request notification, exact-alarm, foreground-service, and device-restart permissions so that scheduled alarms can operate reliably and be restored after a device restart.

Accounts, advertising and selling data

Version 1 of Chalisa Alarm does not require an account and does not include advertising. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed through app settings, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. Because Version 1 does not use an account, local streak and alarm data may be lost if the app is uninstalled or the device is reset or replaced.

Children's privacy

Chalisa Alarm is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Chalisa Alarm adds new features, services, analytics, accounts, cloud backup, advertising, or other data practices.
