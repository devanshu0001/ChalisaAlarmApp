Chalisa Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Chalisa Alarm is an Android alarm app that asks you to recite the Hanuman Chalisa before your alarm is dismissed. It is developed and published by Devanshu Chawla (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

In short: the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not run any servers, and we do not receive, sell or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so it can work:

  • Your alarm settings: time, repeat days, whether the alarm is on, and your choice of alarm sound.
  • Whether you have completed the app’s setup screens.
  • Your recitation streak: the number of days in a row and the date you last completed the Chalisa.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not sent to us.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone access so it can listen while you recite and check your progress through the Hanuman Chalisa. It listens only while you are reciting on the Chalisa screen, after you tap the microphone button.

The app does not record or save audio. Speech recognition is carried out by the speech-recognition service on your device, which on most phones is provided by Google. Depending on your device, its settings and whether you are online, your speech may be sent to that provider to be converted into text. That processing is covered by the provider’s own privacy policy and terms. The recognised words are used only to check the verse you are reciting; they are not stored or sent to us.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone: to check your recitation, as described above.
  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: to show the alarm and open the Chalisa screen when it rings, including over the lock screen.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so the alarm rings at the exact time you set.
  • Foreground service: to keep playing the alarm sound until you complete the Chalisa.
  • Vibration: to vibrate while the alarm rings.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarm again after your phone restarts or the app is updated.

How your data is kept secure

Your settings and streak stay in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s own security, such as your screen lock and storage encryption. The app does not send this information over the internet, and there is no server or database operated by us that holds it. Any speech sent for recognition is handled by your device’s speech-recognition provider under its own security practices.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app’s settings and streak in your device backup (for example, in your Google account) and restore them on a new phone. These backups are managed by Android and Google under their own terms; we cannot access them.

Keeping and deleting your data

The app’s data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → Chalisa Alarm → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. You can also turn off microphone access in your phone’s settings at any time, although the app then cannot check your recitation.

Children’s privacy

Chalisa Alarm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s features or data practices change, for example by adding accounts, cloud backup or analytics, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page.
